Soharab is an authentic, free-style handwriting variable typeface designed by Sohrab Zia. Created directly from physical pen-on-paper handwriting, Soharab captures spontaneous human stroke variation, natural ink flow, and warmth.

The typeface features a two-axis variable architecture with continuous control across Pen Pressure / Weight (wght 100 to 900) and Cursive Slant (slnt -12° to 0°), alongside 8 pre-configured named instances ranging from Thin (Fine Nib) to Black (Heavy Marker).

To contribute, visit github.com/sohrabzia/Soharab-Typeface.
